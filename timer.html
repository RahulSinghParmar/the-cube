How these numbers work

Averages trim the fastest and slowest results: one each for 5 and 12, five each for 100. A remaining DNF makes the average DNF. Interrupted and deleted attempts are excluded. A dash means more solves are needed.

Times include penalties. Larger averages use our practice rules; results are self-recorded.
